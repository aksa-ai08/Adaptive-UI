📖 How to Use This Hub

This Adaptive UI supports multiple ways to interact. Choose what works best for you!

🖐️ Hand Gestures

  • Swipe Left/Right: Move hand sharply to change players (CID Style).
  • Pinch (Thumb + Index): Bring fingers together to Open/Close player details.

🎤 Voice Commands

  • Say "Next" or "Previous" to navigate.
  • Say "Open" or "Close" for details.
  • Say any player name (e.g., "Babar", "Rizwan") to search & open instantly.

⌨️ Keyboard Controls

  • Tab / Shift+Tab: Move focus between cards.
  • Enter / Space: Open selected player details.
  • ← → Arrow Keys: Navigate left/right.
  • Escape: Close modal or guide.

🖱️ Mouse Controls

  • Click: Any player card to view their detailed statistics.
  • Click Outside: Click anywhere on the dark background to close the modal.
  • Hover: See 3D lift and glow effects on cards.
